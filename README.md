One wide horizontal timeline summing up Thursday 19 November 2026 in three stories, morning to night:

1. **Midnight — GTA VI launches.** Grand Theft Auto VI begins its global rollout, returning to Vice City after a 13-year wait, with a 99/100 Metacritic score.
2. **Midday — Leaders meet in Shenzhen.** Presidents Trump, Xi and Putin gather in China for a trilateral summit.
3. **Evening — Record $81.3B IPO.** Anthropic makes the biggest stock-market debut of all time, pricing shares at $83.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) deterministically copies `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to `/output/entry.png`. No network access; running it twice writes identical bytes.

Run: `node main.ts`
